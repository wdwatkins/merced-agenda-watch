## ⚑ Flagged items

**City Council 2026-09-08, item J.5 · Resolution / debt authorization (File 26-721)**
Authorizes accepting and appropriating up to **$50,000,000** in State Revolving Fund (SRF) loan proceeds from the State Water Resources Control Board, deposited to Fund 6000–Wastewater Operating Fund, to finance the **Merced Wastewater Treatment Facility Phase VI Improvement** (Project CP230035). Pooled cash covers project costs until SRF reimbursement. This is the city's largest single debt action in recent memory; repayment is borne by the wastewater enterprise (ratepayers), so it signals future sewer-rate pressure. Interest rate and term are not in the online record — they are in the Construction Installment Sale Agreement attachment. *[big-picture finance]*

**City Council 2026-09-08, item I.6 · Resolution 2026-55 (File 26-697)**
First update to the **City Investment Policy since 2021**, to align with California best practices and the city's stated risk profile and investment objectives; the item text cites expanded institutional/issuer parameters in Section 7, with a redlined version in the packet. Reads as annual-review housekeeping rather than a strategy shift, but it governs how idle city funds are invested. *[big-picture finance]*

**City Council 2026-09-08, item J.1 · Motion (File 26-734)**
Approval of the Council Ad-Hoc Committee's **FY 2026/2027 community funding recommendations** — allocations of city money to outside community organizations. The dollar total and grantee list are not in the item listing (packet check needed). Discretionary spending set by council rather than by staff formula. *[big-picture finance]*

**City Council 2026-09-08, item I.9 · Resolution 2026-56 (File 26-716)**
Declares intent to **vacate the southern portion of Canal (L) Street** between Main (17th) Street and West 16th Street (between Blocks 185 and 186), roughly 14,328 sq ft, and sets a public hearing for **October 5, 2026** (Vacation #26-0002). Driven by the **Bob Hart Square renovation** downtown; vacating the segment folds the right-of-way into the plaza project. Permanently removes a block of street from the downtown grid. *[street design]*
