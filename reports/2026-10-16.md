## ⚑ Flagged items

**City Council 10/19/2026, item J.2 · Action Item**
Staff is asking Council for input and direction on forming a Joint Powers Authority with the Merced County Office of Education to finance infrastructure for the Virginia Smith Trust development — a 650+ acre, already-annexed site near Lake and Cardella Roads south of UC Merced, planned for 3,500+ housing units, 850,000 sq ft of commercial space, parks, and a K-8 school. The financing mechanism is land-secured financing (bonds repaid by future property assessments against the site), governed by the city's A29 policy; no dollar figures are attached yet. This is an open-ended policy-direction item, not a fixed approval, and will shape how financing is structured for one of the city's largest pending growth areas. [Big-picture finance]
